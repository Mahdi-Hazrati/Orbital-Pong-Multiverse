Lose the sidelines.

Your paddle can move around the entire arena. The rim is not a wall. Catch the ball when your paddle glows.

Three real geometries

2D Orbit: XY ball motion inside a circle. A/D moves around all 360 degrees.

3D Sphere: XYZ ball motion inside a sphere. W/S adds the depth angle.

4D Hypersphere: XYZW ball motion inside a four-dimensional ball. Q/E adds the fourth angle. You see a perspective projection, not a literal four-dimensional display.

Assist is on by default: Z and W follow the landing prediction, so you can start with just A/D, mouse or touch. Disable it for full manual control.

Choose your orbit

Campaign: finish each sector's total-return target before losing all lives. Clear sectors in order. Fewer missed returns earn more stars.

Arcade: face 1-3 computer pilots, or play one-life Survival.

Multiplayer: 2-4 local or online pilots take turns. Inactive paddles are ghosts. Miss and lose a life; the last pilot alive wins.

Local touch: touch near your paddle to claim it, then drag. Each finger controls a different paddle. Depth assist makes shared-touch play easier.
